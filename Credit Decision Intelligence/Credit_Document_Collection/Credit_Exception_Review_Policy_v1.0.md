# Credit Exception Review Policy v1.0

**Effective date:** 01-Sep-2026  
**Document type:** Synthetic Demo Exception Policy  

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how BRE failures and referrals are screened for specialist credit review. It supplements, but does not replace, `Credit_Policy_Working_Capital_v1.0.md`.

## 2. Core principle
A BRE failure may be: (a) reviewable through a documented policy-exception process; (b) a referral requiring enhanced professional review; or (c) non-overrideable at the current stage. No AI system may create an exception that is not documented here.

## 3. Reviewable exceptions
### 3.1 DSCR exception
DSCR from **1.10x to 1.24x** may be reviewed by a Senior Credit Risk Specialist when there is no hard-stop conduct flag. Specialist scoring, documented cause, and a credible cash-flow mitigation are required.

### 3.2 Current-ratio exception
Current ratio from **1.00x to 1.09x** may be reviewed together with the overall working-capital cycle when there is no hard-stop conduct flag.

### 3.3 Collateral exception
Realizable collateral coverage from **1.00x to 1.24x** may proceed only with additional collateral or an approved policy exception, consistent with the working-capital policy.

### 3.4 Limited operating-history exception
Operating history from **2.00 to 2.99 completed years** may be reviewed only when all of the following are satisfied: DSCR >=1.25x; commercial bureau score >=700; maximum DPD <=30 days; realizable collateral coverage >=1.25x.

## 4. Referral cases
### 4.1 DPD 31-60 days
Requires a Credit Conduct Specialist review. Endorsement may be considered only when the account is regular at review, there is no DPD above 60 days, and cause/cure evidence is documented.

### 4.2 Single-customer concentration above 40%
Requires enhanced credit review of customer dependency, contract tenure, counterparty quality, alternate pipeline, and mitigation. This is a referral trigger and not automatically a decline.

## 5. Non-overrideable conditions for this synthetic demo
- Any positive suit-filed flag or write-off flag.
- Severe combined conduct weakness where the account is **SMA-2**, maximum DPD is **above 60 days**, and commercial bureau score is **below 700**.

These conditions are not eligible for specialist endorsement at the current stage. Remediation and seasoning are required before reconsideration.

## 6. Exception approval authority
Specialist endorsement is not final sanction. For any policy exception, the applicable approval authority is the **higher of**:
1. the normal sanction authority under `Credit_Policy_Working_Capital_v1.0.md`; and
2. **Regional Credit Committee**.

Exposure above INR 100 million remains subject to Head Office Credit Committee.

## 7. Human control
Only an authorised specialist whose role/scope permits the relevant exception class may complete the specialist score and endorsement. AI may assemble evidence and calculate documented score components, but it must not invent professional-judgement marks.
