# Specialist Review Scorecard Guide v1.0

**Effective date:** 01-Sep-2026  
**Document type:** Synthetic Demo Specialist Guide

## 1. Purpose
The scorecard structures professional judgement for reviewable BRE exceptions and referrals. It does not replace policy thresholds and does not convert a non-overrideable condition into an eligible case.

## 2. Score dimensions
- Financial Resilience: **25 points**
- Repayment & Banking Conduct: **20 points**
- Business Quality: **15 points**
- Management Quality: **10 points**
- Collateral & Security: **10 points**
- Concentration / Sector Risk: **10 points**
- Compensating Factors / Mitigants: **10 points**
- **Total: 100 points**

## 3. Interpretation bands
- **85-100:** Strong exception case; may be endorsed subject to documented conditions.
- **70-84:** Supportable with conditions; may be endorsed by an authorised specialist.
- **55-69:** Enhanced review; do not endorse automatically. Additional evidence or senior escalation is required.
- **Below 55:** Do not endorse.

## 4. Professional judgement rule
Quantitative facts may be pre-populated from authoritative evidence. Judgemental dimensions such as management quality, business quality, and strength of mitigants must be assigned by an authorised professional. AI must never invent missing specialist marks.

## 5. Output
Every completed scorecard must preserve specialist_id, certification/role, application_id, component scores, total score, recommendation, conditions, rationale, and review date.
