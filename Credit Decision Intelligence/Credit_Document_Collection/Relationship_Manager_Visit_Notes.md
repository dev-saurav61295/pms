# Relationship Manager Visit Notes

## C001 - Apex Auto Components Pvt Ltd
**Visit date:** 15-Aug-2026  
**Application:** APP001

The company operates two manufacturing units supplying automotive components to established OEM and Tier-1 customers. Management indicated that confirmed orders for the next two quarters have increased substantially. The company intends to increase raw-material inventory to support the higher order book. Plant utilisation was approximately 78% during the visit. No material operational disruption was observed. Management expects the additional working-capital requirement to continue for approximately 9-12 months. The relationship manager observed satisfactory financial discipline and no material issue was raised during the visit.

Management also referred to FY2026 turnover of approximately INR 440 million during discussion. This is a management statement and was not independently verified in the visit note.

## C002 - BluePeak Textiles Pvt Ltd
**Visit date:** 12-Aug-2026  
**Application:** APP002

Management attributed the recent slowdown to weaker export orders and a prolonged receivable cycle. Inventory was elevated relative to the prior visit. Management expects the next festival-season order cycle to improve utilisation, but no confirmed large order was provided during the visit.

## C003 - Crestline Foods Pvt Ltd

The borrower reported steady demand from regional distributors. Management acknowledged a temporary collection disruption during the prior year and stated that the delayed account was subsequently regularized. The visit note does not independently validate the reason for the delinquency.

## C004 - Delta Engineering Pvt Ltd

## C007 - GreenLeaf Packaging Pvt Ltd

## C008 - Horizon Medical Supplies Pvt Ltd
**Visit date:** 13-Aug-2026  
**Application:** APP008

Horizon Medical Supplies has experienced strong revenue growth over the previous three years. Approximately 58% of current revenue is derived from Medistar Hospitals Group under a major institutional supply arrangement. Management expects the concentration to decline over the next 18 months as three additional hospital networks are onboarded. Formal executed contracts for two of the three new hospital groups were not available during the visit.
