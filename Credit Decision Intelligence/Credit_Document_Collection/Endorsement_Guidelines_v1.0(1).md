# Endorsement Guidelines v1.0

**Effective date:** 01-Sep-2026  
**Document type:** Synthetic Demo Endorsement Guide

## 1. Meaning of endorsement
An endorsement is a documented professional recommendation supporting progression of an application, referral, or policy exception to the next authorised decision stage. It is not final sanction.

## 2. Permitted outcomes
- **ENDORSED**
- **ENDORSED_WITH_CONDITIONS**
- **ENDORSED_POLICY_EXCEPTION**
- **NOT_ENDORSED**
- **INELIGIBLE_FOR_ENDORSEMENT**

## 3. Minimum endorsement record
The endorsement must include: application_id; borrower_id; specialist/reviewer identity; applicable BRE exception/referral; specialist score when required; compensating factors; unresolved risks; conditions; rationale; next authority; date.

## 4. Endorsement gate
A case cannot be endorsed when the exception policy marks the condition as non-overrideable. A case with score below 70 cannot be endorsed under the standard specialist scorecard path without a separately documented senior escalation.

## 5. Authority
Endorsement does not replace sanction authority. The next decision authority must be derived from the working-capital sanction bands plus any higher exception-approval authority required by `Credit_Exception_Review_Policy_v1.0.md`.
