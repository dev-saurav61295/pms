# Credit Policy - Working Capital v1.0

**Effective date:** 01-Apr-2026  
**Document type:** Synthetic Demo Policy  
**Purpose:** One Space Credit Decision Intelligence event demonstration only.

## 1. Eligibility
- Minimum operating history: **3 completed years**.
- Commercial bureau score: **700 or above**.
- Write-off: **None permitted**.
- Suit filed: **None permitted**.
- DPD above 60 days during the previous 12 months: **Not normally eligible**.
- Any DPD above 30 days during the previous 12 months: **Credit Committee referral required**.

## 2. Financial requirements
- DSCR: **1.25x or above**.
- Current Ratio: **1.10x or above**.
- Debt / Equity: **2.50x or below**.

## 3. Banking conduct
- Average facility utilisation up to 90%: Acceptable.
- Average utilisation above 90% and up to 95%: Caution; analyst must comment.
- Average utilisation above 95%: Credit review required.
- Frequent payment or inward returns: investigate and document cause.
- Material account irregularity: credit review required.

## 4. Collateral
- Minimum realizable collateral coverage: **1.25x of proposed exposure**.
- Coverage from 1.00x to 1.24x: may be considered only with additional collateral or an approved policy exception.
- Coverage below 1.00x: Credit Committee exception required.

Realizable Collateral Coverage = Realizable Value / Proposed Exposure.

## 5. Customer concentration
If more than **40% of annual revenue** is derived from a single customer, the application must be referred for **enhanced credit review**. The analyst must evaluate customer dependency, contract tenure, counterparty quality, alternative customer pipeline, and concentration mitigation.

## 6. Revenue validation
GST-reported turnover should normally be within **10%** of audited/projected turnover. Variances above 10% require documented explanation.

## 7. Sanction / review authority
- Proposed Exposure <= INR 50 million: Branch Credit Manager.
- Proposed Exposure > INR 50 million and <= INR 100 million: Regional Credit Committee.
- Proposed Exposure > INR 100 million: Head Office Credit Committee.

## 8. Decision support
Automated or AI-generated outputs are advisory. Final sanction, decline, exception approval and conditions must be issued by an authorized human authority.
