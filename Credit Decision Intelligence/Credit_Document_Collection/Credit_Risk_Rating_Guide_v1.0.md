# Credit Risk Rating Guide v1.0

**Effective date:** 01-Apr-2026  
**Document type:** Synthetic Demo Risk Guide

This guide supports qualitative risk communication and does not replace individual policy tests.

## Strong indicators
- Multi-year revenue and profitability growth.
- DSCR comfortably above policy minimum.
- Clean repayment conduct.
- Commercial bureau score materially above minimum.
- Collateral coverage above policy minimum.
- Low customer concentration.

## Watch indicators
- Deteriorating revenue or profitability.
- DSCR or liquidity close to policy floor.
- Average utilisation above 90%.
- Repeated payment returns.
- Rising external exposure.
- Single-customer concentration above the policy threshold.

## Severe indicators
- Material repayment delinquency.
- Negative profitability combined with weakening liquidity.
- Bureau score below minimum.
- Collateral coverage below 1.00x.
- Multiple simultaneous policy failures.

## Interpretation rule
Risk labels must be explained using underlying evidence. A label by itself is not a lending decision.
