# Event Demo Script - Sales & Distribution Intelligence

**Target duration:** 8-10 minutes  
**Primary story:** East Region August 2026 decline

## 0:00-1:00 - Enterprise health
**Ask:** `Give me an executive summary of current Sales & Distribution health.`

Expected experience: identify West as commercially strongest, East as the major decline, promotion differences, inventory/availability risks and Pune service deterioration. Do not reveal all ground-truth detail immediately.

## 1:00-3:00 - Main root-cause investigation
**Ask:** `Sales in the East Region declined significantly this month. Why?`

Expected storyline:
- August secondary sales INR 21.264M vs July INR 24.000M: -11.4%.
- Estimated identified impact: stock availability 42%, outlet coverage 26%, promotion rollout 19%, returns/damage/expiry 13%.
- Show that different distributors fail for different reasons.

## 3:00-4:00 - Prioritize intervention
**Ask:** `Which distributors should I intervene with first?`

Expected priority pattern:
1. MetroLink Consumer Trade - severe stock-outs, healthy coverage/demand signal.
2. BlueRiver Wholesale - coverage/productive outlet collapse despite acceptable availability.
3. Utkal Consumer Distribution - primary strong, secondary weak, inventory/ageing building.

## 4:00-5:00 - Cross-source context
**Ask:** `What do the field notes and distributor reviews say about MetroLink?`

Expected: repeated replenishment requests, key FizzUp/HydraGo shortages, outlets with demand but inability to replenish.

## 5:00-6:00 - Hidden channel risk
**Ask:** `Which distributors look healthy from primary sales but have underlying risk?`

Expected: Utkal. Explain sell-in versus sell-through and inventory build-up.

## 6:00-7:00 - Promotion intelligence
**Ask:** `Which promotions are actually working, and which are not?`

Expected contrast:
- FizzUp Summer Splash: execution ~93%, uplift +28%, positive economics.
- PureGlow Glow Days: execution ~94%, uplift +3%, weak economics.
- CrunchCo Monsoon Combo: execution ~48%, participating outlets +24%; rollout problem, not scheme failure.

## 7:00-8:00 - Emerging risk despite strong sales
**Ask:** `Which currently strong area should management worry about?`

Expected: Pune/West. Sales are strong, but fill rate, OTIF, cancellations and delivery time deteriorate.

## 8:00-9:00 - Conflicting evidence
**Ask:** `Do the service metrics and field observations agree for Horizon Distribution Services?`

Expected: no. Monthly fill/OTIF look acceptable while late-month priority-outlet notes report delays. Surface the conflict and recommend targeted investigation.

## 9:00-10:00 - Management action
**Ask:** `What are the five actions management should take this week?`

Expected action themes:
1. Restore high-velocity beverage availability at MetroLink.
2. Recover route/coverage execution at BlueRiver.
3. Review Utkal replenishment and ageing risk.
4. Expand CrunchCo rollout where execution is missing; do not abandon a locally effective scheme.
5. Correct Pune fulfilment deterioration before it affects next-month sales.

### Closing prompt
`Explain the East Region decline in one board-ready paragraph.`

Use this to demonstrate that One Space is performing evidence orchestration and root-cause synthesis rather than dashboard lookup or generic RAG Q&A.
